🦸 Our Story

Every pet deserves a hero.

Pet Hero started with one idea: grooming shouldn't be stressful for pets or their people. So we brought the salon to you.

The Van

A full grooming salon, parked outside your door.

Our custom-fitted van carries everything a proper grooming salon does — warm water, professional dryers, grooming tables and all the tools our specialists need. No stuffy waiting rooms, no stressful car rides for your pet: we simply come to you.

Our Approach

Natural products. Breed-specific care.

We only use natural, pet-safe shampoos and treatments — gentle enough for sensitive skin and safe for cats and dogs of every age. Every groom is tailored to the breed and coat in front of us, never a one-size-fits-all cut.

Certified Specialists

EU-certified, genuinely animal-loving groomers.

Every groomer on the team is trained and certified to European grooming standards — but what really shows is the patience they bring to every appointment, especially with nervous first-timers.
