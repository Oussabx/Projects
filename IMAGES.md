# Adding images to the site

Drop your photos into the matching folder below using **any** common name (jpg/png/webp) — you don't need to match exact filenames. When you send them over (with a note on where each one goes, or just "these are for the gallery"), I'll wire them into the actual pages and remove the placeholder graphics.

| Folder | Used on | What to put there |
|---|---|---|
| `assets/images/hero/` | Home page hero banner | 1 wide photo — the van in action, or a great shot of a groomed pet |
| `assets/images/about/` | About page | Photos of the van, the groomer(s) at work, before/after shots |
| `assets/images/services/` | Services page | Optional — one photo per service (bath, haircut, nail trim, etc.) |
| `assets/images/gallery/` | Gallery page | As many as you want — this becomes the main photo grid |
| `assets/images/team/` | About page | Headshots or candid photos of the groomer(s) |
| `assets/images/reviews/` | Reviews page | Optional — client/pet photos next to testimonials |

## What to send along with the images

- Any inspiration sites/screenshots you like (layout, colors, vibe)
- A short brief: tone (fun/premium/minimal), must-have sections, any copy you already have (services list with prices, real testimonials, etc.)
- Your logo file, if you have a vector/high-res version — otherwise I'll keep the current illustrated wordmark

Once I have these I'll redesign the pages around them rather than the current placeholder layout.
